Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026
Refund Policy Flight Tickets Hotel Bookings Visa Services Holiday Packages Processing Time

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation policies of the respective airline, hotel, or travel service provider.

Flight Tickets

Refunds are processed according to the airline's cancellation and refund policy.

Hotel Bookings

Hotel cancellations are subject to the individual property's cancellation terms.

Visa Services

Government fees, embassy charges, and visa processing fees are generally non-refundable once the application has been submitted. Service charges may also be non-refundable after work has commenced.

Holiday Packages

Cancellation charges vary depending on the package and supplier policies.

Processing Time

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed using the original payment method. The time taken for the amount to reflect depends on the payment gateway, banks, and the respective travel service provider.
