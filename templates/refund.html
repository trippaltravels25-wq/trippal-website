Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026
Refund Policy Flight Tickets Hotel Bookings Visa Services Holiday Packages Processing Time Cancellation & Refund Requests

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation policies of the respective airline, hotel, or travel service provider.

Flight Tickets

Refunds are processed according to the airline's cancellation and refund policy.

Hotel Bookings

Hotel cancellations are subject to the individual property's cancellation terms.

Visa Services

Government fees, embassy charges, and visa processing fees are generally non-refundable once the application has been submitted. Service charges may also be non-refundable after work has commenced.

Holiday Packages

Cancellation charges vary depending on the package and supplier policies.

Processing Time

Where a refund is approved, it will be initiated to the original payment method within 7 business days of approval. The time taken for the refund to reflect in the customer's account may vary depending on the payment gateway, bank, card issuer, and the respective travel service provider.

Cancellation & Refund Requests

Customers may request a cancellation or refund by contacting TripPal Global Travel & Tourism at hussain@trippalglobal.com or +91 89282 63921. Requests should include the booking or reference number and relevant traveller details. Cancellation and refund eligibility will be determined according to the applicable terms and cancellation policies of the airline, hotel, visa service provider, holiday package, or other travel service provider.
